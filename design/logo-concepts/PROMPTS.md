# Split Stack retained logo briefs

The logo directions were generated with the built-in image tool. The in-game
practice screenshot was the authoritative reference for tile colors, patterns,
and embedded-special iconography.

## Shared identity

- Eight equal tiles form the Hollow Cross at `(3,1)`, `(3,2)`, `(1,3)`,
  `(2,3)`, `(4,3)`, `(5,3)`, `(3,4)`, and `(3,5)` on a 5-by-5 grid.
- The empty `(3,3)` cell is square, with uniform horizontal and vertical pitch.
- Tile identities are cyan diagonal, purple crosses, blue vertical, green left
  chevrons, red right chevrons, orange horizontal, yellow dots, and pale-lemon
  single cross.
- Outer tips contain Barrier at the top, Garbage Core at the left, Blackout at
  the right, and Column Bomb at the bottom.
- The mark is icon-only, mask-safe, and contains no outer app-icon frame.

## Selected: Retro Aqua

Render the shared identity in a polished late-2000s glossy skeuomorphic style:
translucent gel tiles, rounded bevels, white specular bands, saturated colors,
recessed icon plaques, soft shadows, and a deep blue-black radial background.
Include no operating-system branding and no diagonal seam or background split.

## Retained: Dark Diagonal Field

Use a polished semi-flat neon tile treatment. Divide the background from
bottom-left to top-right with a quiet, unlit boundary: cool blue-black above and
warm purple-black below. Do not add a luminous seam.

## Retained: Modern Flat

Use contemporary flat vector treatment on a uniform near-black navy background:
solid fills, simplified darker patterns, crisp uniform strokes, modest corners,
and flat dark power-icon insets. Avoid bevels, glass, bloom, strong shadows,
gradients, and diagonal divisions.

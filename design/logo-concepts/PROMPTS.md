# Split Stack retained logo briefs

The logo directions were generated with the built-in image tool. The in-game
practice screenshot was the authoritative reference for tile colors, patterns,
and embedded-special iconography.

## Selected: Solid Charcoal Marked Cells

The selected logo uses a uniform `#111827` charcoal-navy background constructed
directly in the SVG master. No generated imagery is used in its background.

`05b-ambient-mesh-cell-master.svg` deterministically composites the approved
Hollow Cross over that solid color:

- Eight equal 160 px cells occupy `(3,1)`, `(3,2)`, `(1,3)`, `(2,3)`,
  `(4,3)`, `(5,3)`, `(3,4)`, and `(3,5)` on a 170 px-pitch 5-by-5 grid.
- The complete `(3,3)` grid slot remains absent, locking square center space.
- The four inner cells retain the canonical T, S, Z, and O colors and patterns.
- The four outer cells use the in-game marked-cell presentation: canonical
  accent surface, no piece pattern, ivory glyph with dark understroke, and a
  restrained resting-state rim glow.
- Marked tips remain Barrier at top, Garbage Core at left, Blackout at right,
  and Column Bomb at bottom.

## Retained: Ambient Mesh Marked Cells

The retained ambient-mesh background plate used the built-in image tool with
this prompt:

> Create a square, edge-to-edge ambient-mesh app-icon background with a deep
> midnight navy base, a restrained cyan atmospheric bloom in the upper-left,
> a restrained violet atmospheric bloom in the lower-right, a quieter dark
> center, gentle corner vignette, and exceptionally fine grain. Include no
> logo, cross, blocks, cells, symbols, text, objects, frame, diagonal split,
> seam, or central spotlight ring.

## Retained directions: shared identity

- Eight equal tiles form the Hollow Cross at `(3,1)`, `(3,2)`, `(1,3)`,
  `(2,3)`, `(4,3)`, `(5,3)`, `(3,4)`, and `(3,5)` on a 5-by-5 grid.
- The empty `(3,3)` cell is square, with uniform horizontal and vertical pitch.
- In the retained pre-2026 directions, tile identities are cyan diagonal,
  purple crosses, blue vertical, green left
  chevrons, red right chevrons, orange horizontal, yellow dots, and pale-lemon
  single cross.
- Outer tips contain Barrier at the top, Garbage Core at the left, Blackout at
  the right, and Column Bomb at the bottom.
- The mark is icon-only, mask-safe, and contains no outer app-icon frame.

## Retained: Retro Aqua

Render the shared identity in a polished late-2000s glossy skeuomorphic style:
translucent gel tiles, rounded bevels, white specular bands, saturated colors,
recessed icon plaques, soft shadows, and a deep blue-black radial background.
Include no operating-system branding and no diagonal seam or background split.

## Retained: Dark Diagonal Field

Use a polished semi-flat neon tile treatment. Divide the background from
bottom-left to top-right with a quiet, unlit boundary: cool blue-black above and
warm purple-black below. Do not add a luminous seam.

## Retained: Modern Flat

Use contemporary flat vector treatment on a uniform near-black navy background:
solid fills, simplified darker patterns, crisp uniform strokes, modest corners,
and flat dark power-icon insets. Avoid bevels, glass, bloom, strong shadows,
gradients, and diagonal divisions.
